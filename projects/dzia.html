Dzia

Dzia, 2D pixel game

C++SFML

About

A top-down pixel action game about a hero sent to the ruins of an old castle to defeat a monster that terrorizes the nearby villages. The battle is split into three waves: jellyfish, then baby dragons, then the boss. Between waves the player picks a card that changes the rules of the fight.

I wrote it entirely in C++ with SFML, with no game engine, as a university project. Every system, from the game loop to the menus and saves, is my own code.

What it includes

  • Three-phase fight with a boss and its own health bar at the end
  • Card choice between phases that trades risk for power, for example tougher enemies for more damage
  • Enemies that patrol their area and chase the hero once they get close
  • Shop run by a frog merchant, with coins earned for clearing each wave
  • Lava zones that hurt the hero and invisible walls for map collisions
  • Sprite-sheet animation for walking, attacks, enemies and the intro scroll
  • Main menu, pause menu, death screen and a save system that keeps the three latest saves
