Cog

Cog Chain, 3D logic puzzle

About

A 3D logic puzzle for Windows and Android that I designed and developed as a personal project, with all code in C#.

GodotC#UMLDoxygen

    How to play

    Place gears on the available axes, connect them to the motor, and get the target mechanism spinning. Gears mesh automatically when placed at the right distance. Remove a misplaced gear and try again, but the fewer moves you make, the better your score. Each level is rated from 1 to 5 stars based on your time and number of moves. Complete a level with at least 1 star to unlock the next one.

    Features

  • 18 levels with increasing complexity: more gears, more axes, tighter puzzles
  • Realistic gear physics: gears rotate in opposite directions, speed depends on tooth ratio
    • Multiple mechanism types: doors, lamps, clock hands, keys and more 5-star rating system with individual time and move thresholds per level Replay any level to improve your score Global leaderboard ranked by total stars across all levels Accounts with local save data and optional Firebase sync across devices 9 achievements to unlock Music and SFX volume controls English and Polish language support

    What it includes

    • Documentation with UML diagrams and a player guide
    • Code documentation generated with Doxygen
