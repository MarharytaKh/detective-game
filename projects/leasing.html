Leasing

Leasing database in Oracle

OraclePL/SQLSQL

About

A database for a leasing company that stores clients, leased items, contracts, installment schedules and payments. The business logic lives in the database itself: a PL/SQL package calculates installments, books payments and tracks arrears, and a trigger keeps the history of contract statuses.

The scripts run in order on Oracle 19c or in Oracle Live SQL: schema, PL/SQL, test data and reports.

What it includes

  • Six tables with keys, CHECK constraints and indexes for the most common queries
  • Annuity installment schedule from item value, down payment, interest rate and term, split into principal and interest
  • Payment booking: a payment closes its installment, and the last one closes the contract
  • Arrears control: overdue installments are flagged, and three or more move the contract to collections
  • Contract status history logged by a trigger
  • Reports with window functions: client portfolio, arrears, cumulative monthly receipts, portfolio share by category and a contract ranking
