Reversi

Reversi, console game

C++

About

The classic board game Reversi (Othello) as a C++ console application with the interface in Polish. You can play against a friend on the same computer or against a computer opponent, and every result goes into a ranking of players that is kept between sessions.

It was a university project built around object-oriented design. Both kinds of players share one abstract base class, so the game loop does not care whether a move comes from a person or the computer.

What it includes

  • Two modes: player vs player and player vs computer
  • Full move rules: legal moves are checked in all eight directions, and the possible moves are marked on the board before each turn
  • Captured pieces flip automatically, and the score is updated after every move
  • A greedy computer opponent that picks the move capturing the most pieces, choosing at random between equally good moves
  • End of game detection when the board is full or neither player can move, with a winner or a draw
  • Player statistics saved to a file: wins, losses and win rate, sorted into a top-5 ranking
  • Class hierarchy with inheritance and virtual methods: an abstract player with human and computer subclasses
